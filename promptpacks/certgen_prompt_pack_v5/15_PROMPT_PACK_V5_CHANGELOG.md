# CertGen Prompt Pack V5 Changelog

## V5 Identity

V5 is the **CVPR-ready-except-runs** pack.

It upgrades from V4's real-pilot readiness into a nearly complete CVPR paper/release machine while preserving the no-evidence boundary.

## New in V5

- V5 state intake and gap audit.
- Paper identity lock.
- Machine-readable claim contract.
- Forbidden/allowed pre-run claims.
- Related-work board with citation verification status.
- Preregistration and analysis-plan lock.
- Result contracts for paper tables and figures.
- Main CVPR paper scaffold.
- Supplement/proof/statistical appendix scaffold.
- Reproducibility artifact capsule.
- Anonymity/privacy/release scan.
- Safe execution runbooks and command bundles.
- Result injection and claim trace system.
- Reviewer simulator and author response bank.
- CVPR readiness scorecard and kill list.
- V5 final audit and handoff.

## Preserved Boundaries

V5 does not:

- produce real benchmark results;
- produce a real undecided fraction;
- claim ranking movement;
- claim compute savings;
- claim rigorous FID certification;
- promote smoke/dry-run/synthetic artifacts to evidence.

## Expected Final State

After V5:

> CertGen should be CVPR-ready-except-runs, not CVPR-submission-ready.

The next step is real execution.

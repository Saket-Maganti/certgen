pass the paper gate. The registered types are: headline certified partial
ranking, anytime trajectory, decidedness versus budget, censored
samples-to-decision, protocol sensitivity, resource-use consequences, visual model-pair
gallery, and controlled-failure gallery.

hashes, claim-gate status, caption metadata, and limitations. Representative
images illustrate a case; they do not prove a distribution-level claim.

\begin{abstract}
Generative-model comparisons rely on finite-sample metric estimates, yet a point
estimate does not establish a direction that remains valid under continuous
monitoring. CertGen is a proposed decision and audit layer for a declared model
pair, reference distribution, feature pipeline, bounded kernel, and sample
budget. Its claim-capable core uses non-overlapping disjoint-pair contributions
for the difference of two RBF-MMD-squared discrepancies and a time-uniform
union-Hoeffding confidence sequence. The guarantee is conditional on mutually
independent IID model and reference samples, a prospectively fixed extractor,
preprocessing rule, kernel bandwidth, stopping rule, and comparison family.
Bonferroni correction is the current conservative family-level protocol.
The planned vision audit reports resolved and unresolved edges as certified
partial rankings across prospectively frozen Inception, CLIP, and DINO-style
feature spaces, together with censored samples-to-decision rather than a forced
total leaderboard.
Betting-grid confidence intervals and e-BH outputs remain diagnostic until their
directional and adaptive-stopping proof obligations are discharged. FID and
polynomial KID remain descriptive. No real visual generative-model audit has been run:

\end{abstract}

\section{Experimental Protocol}

Benchmarks and model pairs are selected prospectively from the CVPR benchmark,
model, comparison, and feature-space registries, and are frozen before the
affected outcomes are inspected. Each run requires released samples or
reproducible checkpoints, a reference-draw plan, an immutable preprocessing and
extractor lock, validated feature caches, metric reproduction checks, null and
obvious-gap controls, and a frozen Bonferroni family before a non-claim
certificate pilot. The planned feature-specific outputs are certified edges,
unresolved pairs, censored samples-to-decision, and disagreements across
Inception, CLIP, and DINO-style representations. A total ranking is never
imputed from missing or unresolved edges.

preselected contestable model pairs. The endpoint is not yet measured:

\section{Limitations, Ethics, and Reproducibility}

CertGen does not replace metrics, human evaluation, or benchmark design. It asks a narrower question: whether a declared metric comparison is decided under the run contract. Before real runs, empirical strength is blocked.

FID and FD-style metrics are descriptive in this scaffold. A naive mean-based confidence sequence does not directly certify FID because FID is a nonlinear functional of sample mean and covariance.

The release plan is zero-cost by default: no paid APIs, paid GPUs, paid datasets, paid annotation, or hosted inference are required by tests or dry-run commands.

\section{Method}

\subsection{Problem Setup}
For models $A$ and $B$ and reference set $R$, CertGen tracks $\Delta=d(A,R)-d(B,R)$ under a declared metric, preprocessing lock, and sample budget.

\subsection{Metric Capability Boundary}
The surrounding protocol can register many descriptive metrics, but the current
claim-capable certificate is not metric-agnostic. It is limited to the
implemented bounded RBF-kernel stream described below.

\subsection{Clean-Core Stream}
The clean core uses non-overlapping disjoint pairs from mutually independent IID
samples from $A$, $B$, and $R$. Each unit is the difference between linear-time
RBF-MMD-squared contributions for $(A,R)$ and $(B,R)$. Feature normalization and
a prospectively fixed RBF bandwidth are part of the estimand. No empirical
clipping is used to manufacture boundedness.

\subsection{Anytime-Valid Decision Certificate}
The primary certificate monitors a union-Hoeffding confidence sequence for the
bounded stream and stops when the interval excludes zero, or reports
not-decided at the maximum budget. This route is time-uniform under the stated
sampling and protocol assumptions. The finite-grid betting interval remains an
engineering diagnostic and is not claim-capable.

\subsection{Multiple Comparisons and Dependence}
A leaderboard audit contains many decisions and may reuse a reference pool. The
primary protocol predeclares the family and applies Bonferroni correction to the
time-uniform pairwise certificates. Point-null e-values and e-BH are reported
only as exploratory diagnostics until direction and cross-stream adaptive
stopping are covered by a proof. Pairwise certificates are not promoted to a
global total ranking.

\subsection{FID/FD Policy}
FID and FD-style metrics are descriptive unless a separate rigorous functional treatment is implemented and audited.

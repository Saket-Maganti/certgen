## Ceiling verdict

The current repository is suitable for no submission. A complete minimum study could support AISTATS, WACV, or TMLR consideration depending on the outcome and emphasis. A NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR/CVPR main-track attempt requires the maximum empirical program and remains risky because the inference ingredients are prior art and the direct generative-comparison problem has recent overlap. JMLR/TPAMI require a substantially deeper contribution than the current core.

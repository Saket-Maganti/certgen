\section{MMD, KID, and CMMD Streams}

The claim-capable clean-core certificate targets the implemented
non-overlapping disjoint-pair difference of two RBF-MMD-squared estimators.
Feature extraction, preprocessing, normalization, and bandwidth are locked
before the stream is observed. Polynomial KID and unimplemented CMMD variants
do not inherit this guarantee. Empirical clipping is diagnostic only unless the
clipped estimand is prospectively defined and scientifically justified.

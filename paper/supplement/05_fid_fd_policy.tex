\section{FID and FD Policy}

FID is a nonlinear biased functional of sample mean and covariance. A naive mean-based confidence sequence does not directly certify FID. CertGen's rigorous clean-core certificate applies only to metrics with a valid stream or estimator structure. FID and FD-style quantities are descriptive, block-experimental, or future work unless a watertight method is added and audited.

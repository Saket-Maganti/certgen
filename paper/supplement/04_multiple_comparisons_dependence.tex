\section{Multiple Comparisons and Dependence}

Batch audits report the prospectively declared family and dependence
diagnostics. The primary implemented policy is Bonferroni correction over
time-uniform pairwise certificates. A terminal betting e-value for the exact
point null may be valid for one fixed stream, and e-BH can control FDR for valid
e-values under arbitrary dependence, but the present implementation has not
proved directional certification or globally adaptive stopping across the
family. Its e-BH output is therefore exploratory and claim-blocked. Reused
reference samples, feature caches, or model outputs are recorded explicitly.

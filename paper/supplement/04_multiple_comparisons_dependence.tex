\section{Multiple Comparisons and Dependence}

Batch audits report the multiplicity policy and dependence diagnostics. Per-comparison terminal e-values from the betting e-process are combined with e-BH (Wang and Ramdas, 2022), which controls FDR under arbitrary dependence; this is the relevant regime because comparisons share one reference set. Reused reference samples, feature caches, or model outputs are additionally surfaced as caveats rather than silently treated as independent evidence.

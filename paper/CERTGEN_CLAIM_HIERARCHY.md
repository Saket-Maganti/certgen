# CertGen Claim Hierarchy

Status: `PRE_RUN_CONTRACT`; `claim_allowed=false`.

## Primary proposed claim

> A prospectively locked bounded-RBF generative-model comparison can be monitored continuously and reported as a multiplicity-adjusted directional certificate or an honest censored unresolved outcome, and a real audit can quantify how this changes point-estimate conclusions.

The first clause is conditional on the documented stream assumptions. The second is completely blocked until the registered real study runs.

## Empirical claims

All are `MISSING` and forbidden in present tense:

- any model is closer to CIFAR-10 than another;
- any real comparison is decided or unresolved;
- a measured undecided fraction;
- measured samples-to-decision or compute savings;
- a real partial ranking;
- protocol-dependent reversals;
- checkpoint, generation, extractor, or runtime success; and
- generalization across benchmarks.

## Scope claims

The current inferential scope is a fixed bounded RBF kernel over immutable feature vectors, a declared reference population, disjoint model draw identities, an IID-with-replacement draw plan over a frozen empirical reference population, union-Hoeffding monitoring, and Bonferroni families. CLIP-feature RBF MMD is in scope only after exact model/revision/preprocessing and numerical reproduction pass.

## Explicit non-claims

- FID and FD-DINOv2 are descriptive under current code.
- Polynomial KID does not inherit bounded-RBF guarantees.
- CertGen is not metric-agnostic at the inferential level.
- Unresolved does not imply equivalent.
- Decided does not imply a practically meaningful effect.
- Metric closeness does not establish universal model quality.
- One benchmark does not establish generality.
- Pairwise certificates do not force a total ranking.
- Retrospective stopping on a completed cache does not prove realized GPU savings.
- Unit tests, simulations, notebooks, manifests, and static audits are not model evidence.

## Claim-promotion rule

A manuscript claim may move from placeholder to allowed only when the exact source, model pair, reference, extractor, metric specification, sample budget, cache hashes, reference draw-plan hash, family manifest, certificate, and aggregation artifact all appear in the claim-evidence ledger with a passing independent paper gate. No current row qualifies.

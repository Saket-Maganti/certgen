# Reviewer Attacks V1

- Just KID with a stopping rule: V2 must show why optional-stopping validity is load-bearing.
- FID certificate invalid: V1 agrees and keeps FID descriptive.
- Stats paper, not vision: future work needs vision-native audits, galleries, and preprocessing discipline.
- No audit headline: V1 has no audit run; the first pilot must settle whether the headline exists.
- Template fatigue: framing must emphasize the generative-vision audit and usable certificate artifact.
- Sample availability: registry templates require released samples, checkpoints, or feature stats before a pilot.
- Preprocessing mismatch: every future feature manifest must record resize and interpolation policy.

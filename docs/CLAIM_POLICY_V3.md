# Claim Policy V3

`NO_REAL_EVIDENCE`

V3 can say:

- infrastructure is implemented;
- dry-run plans were generated;
- feature caches passed or failed validation;
- a pilot was computed in non-claim mode if validated features were supplied.

V3 cannot say:

- model superiority;
- published-result error;
- benchmark audit conclusions;
- decidedness fraction as a paper claim;
- leaderboard movement.

Forbidden claim strings in non-claim artifacts include:

- we show that
- our results demonstrate
- model A is better
- published wins are undecided
- X% of claims fail
- ranking changes

A real pilot is claim-eligible only after all provenance, feature-cache, metric-reproduction, certificate, replay, and explicit claim-eligibility gates pass.

# Partial-Ranking Provenance

Every direct edge names its certificate path and SHA-256, comparison, feature space, family, study, and `direct` status. Transitive implications are labeled `transitive` and list their supporting certificate artifacts; they are not presented as direct measurements. Unresolved, invalid, contradictory, and incomparable pairs remain explicit.

Cross-feature consensus is prospective: matching decided directions permit a consensus edge; opposite directions produce disagreement; decided in one and unresolved in another remains unresolved; any invalid lane blocks consensus. CertGen never forces consensus across representations.

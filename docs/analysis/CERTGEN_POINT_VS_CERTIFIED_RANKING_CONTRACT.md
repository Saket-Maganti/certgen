# Point Estimate Versus Certified Ranking Contract

The canonical comparison artifact contains `point_estimate_total_order`, `certified_direct_edges`, `transitive_implications`, `unresolved_pairs`, and `invalid_pairs`.

Point estimates are descriptive and may form a displayed total order. Certified conclusions remain a partial graph under the frozen family-wise error allocation. Transitive implications are distinguished from directly certified edges. Unresolved or invalid pairs cannot be filled from the point leaderboard.
